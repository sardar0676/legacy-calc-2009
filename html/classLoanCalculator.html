Detailed Description

A simple loan calculator for computing EMIs, interest rates, loan balances, and related calculations.

Supports calculation of:

  • Loan balance after N payments
  • Monthly payment
  • Number of payments
  • Original loan amount
  • Interest rate (approximate)
  • Effective interest rate (including fees)
